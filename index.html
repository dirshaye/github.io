About

Hi, I am Dirshaye.

I am a Software Engineer at Emma — The Sleep Company in Lisbon, where I contribute to backend microservices, event-driven workflows, and platform architectures within the Operations Backbone team, supporting internal logistics and warehouse automation. In my production work, I build with Go, TypeScript/Fastify, PostgreSQL, AWS (SQS, EC2, S3), Docker, Kubernetes, and Grafana.

I care deeply about software quality end to end: clean API contracts, observability, robust error handling, and performance tuning that holds up reliably under real-world usage.
